Sustainability & regenerative living

A house that costs less to run is worth more to live in.

Solar-ready homes, fruit gardens, clean water systems, and thoughtful upgrades that give back to the land and the community. Here is what that means in practice.

The case, in plain words

Two houses can look identical from the street and cost very different amounts to live in. One runs the air conditioner all day against a hot, uninsulated attic. The other has shade where the sun hits, insulation where it was missing, and a roof that's ready for panels. The second house gives its owner money back every month, quietly, for as long as they live there.

That is what we mean by regenerative. Not a label. A house that produces some of what it consumes: shade, water for the garden, power, food. Every one of those pieces is ordinary work done on purpose, and every one lowers a bill.

It also matters for resilience. The Valley gets sustained heat, high humidity, hard water and a hurricane season. A house built to cost less to run is usually also the house that holds up better when the weather turns.

What we actually do

Shade and insulation first

The cheapest cooling is the heat that never gets in. Attic insulation, air sealing, weatherstripping, window shading and the right trees on the west side come before anything with a plug.

Water for the garden

Rain catchment for beds and trees, and drought-tolerant planting where grass was burning up. Anything connected to the house's own plumbing is done by a licensed plumber.

Solar-ready

Conduit, roof and panel prepared so panels can go on when it makes sense for the owner, without opening walls again. Electrical work is performed by licensed subcontractors.

Something that grows food

A citrus tree, a raised bed, herbs by the kitchen door. Small, cheap, and the thing people mention first when they talk about the house.

Materials that last

Repairs done with what survives the climate: rot-resistant wood, proper flashing, sealed penetrations. Fixing it twice costs more than fixing it right.

You keep it up yourself

We show owners how to maintain and upgrade what we put in. A house nobody understands doesn't stay efficient for long.

Already in a house?

You don't have to buy from us to get this. Most of these upgrades can be done one at a time, in an afternoon, in the house you're in now. Tell us which bill you'd like to be smaller and we'll start there.
