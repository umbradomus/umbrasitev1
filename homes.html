What Umbra looks for

Houses in Cameron County and the Valley that other buyers have passed over: the ones that need real work, not the ones that need staging. Good bones, a fixable problem, a street worth living on.

We open the house up, find out what's actually wrong, and fix that first. Then the things that make it cheaper to live in: shade, insulation, water for the garden, solar-ready wiring, something that grows food.

What a buyer gets

A house that was fixed properly, and the proof: every job photographed before, during and after, so you can see what was behind the walls and what was done about it.

A house that costs less to run than the one next door, and a walkthrough of how to keep it that way.

A straight answer, in writing, on time. The same promise we make on every repair: We reply within 2 hours, 7am–9pm, seven days a week. If we're late, we tell you why in writing and you go first the next morning.
